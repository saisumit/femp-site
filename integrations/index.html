Shopify

Femp reads orders, products and inventory to work out days of cover, sales and repeat buyers, and to compare ad spend with real sales. With your approval it can improve product titles, descriptions and image alt text, and publish blog posts, which helps your products show up on Google and in AI assistants. See how products get found in ChatGPT.

Amazon Seller Central

Femp reads your Amazon orders and FBA stock alongside Shopify, so days of cover reflects everything you sell, not just one channel.

Meta Ads and Google Ads

Read-only. Femp checks each morning whether yesterday's spend turned into real store sales and suggests the budget change it would make. You make any change yourself in the ads manager.

Google Search Console

Search data feeds the afternoon findability check: which searches find you, which pages are slipping, and what to fix first.

Gmail

Connect the inbox your store uses and Femp checks it every hour for emails that cost money if missed: chargebacks with deadlines, supplier delays, marketplace account warnings and reviews. It drafts replies; you send them.
