What Finance handles for you

  • Watchlist summary

    Earnings dates, big price moves and the news behind them, for the stocks you choose.

  • Filings in plain words

    Quarterly and annual reports summed up: revenue, margins and what changed in the outlook.

  • Bills due

    Card and utility bills found in your Gmail, with a reminder before the due date.

  • Subscriptions you don't use

    Recurring charges you haven't touched in a while, so you can cancel them.

  • Ask anything

    “What did Orbit say about margins?” or “How much did I spend on apps this year?”

  • Research only

    No trades, no buy or sell calls. You make every decision.

Femp Finance is research and reminders, not financial advice. It never places trades or tells you what to buy or sell.

Connects: Your watchlist · Gmail (bills and receipts). Each connection uses the platform's own sign-in, and nothing is sent without your yes.
