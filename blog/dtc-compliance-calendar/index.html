The DTC compliance calendar: sales tax, 1099s, SMS consent and the dates that cost money

Almost none of these deadlines live in the tools you open every day. Each one has a date, a penalty or a lawsuit behind it. Here they are on one page.

By the Femp team · November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

● Not legal or tax advice

This is a checklist of what to ask your accountant and lawyer about, written for US brands. Thresholds and dates change; confirm the ones that apply to your business with them.

A $2M brand has more filing obligations than most owners realize: federal and state taxes, state entity reports, contractor forms, and product, marketing and privacy rules that depend on what you sell and where. Your accountant knows the tax dates. Usually nobody owns the rest.

Sales tax: nexus and filing

  • Economic nexus. Since the 2018 Wayfair decision, states can require you to collect once your sales into them cross a threshold. Most states use $100,000 a year (some add a transaction count); California and Texas use $500,000, and New York uses $500,000 plus 100 transactions. In some states, marketplace sales count toward the threshold even though Amazon collects the tax.
  • Register before you collect. Collecting sales tax without a permit is a problem of its own.
  • Who remits. Amazon collects and remits as a marketplace facilitator. Shopify calculates tax but does not file or remit it for you.
  • Zero returns. Once registered, file on the schedule the state assigns, even when you owe nothing.

Federal tax dates

  • Estimated taxes: April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15. Set cash aside each month.
  • S-corp and partnership returns (1120-S, 1065): mid-March for calendar-year businesses. Late filing penalties are charged per owner, per month.
  • Payroll deposits and Form 941 quarterly. Missed payroll taxes can be assessed against owners personally.

1099s and W-9s

Collect a W-9 from every contractor and agency before you pay them. The 1099-NEC reporting threshold rises from $600 to $2,000 for payments made in 2026, and the federal 1099-K threshold is back to $20,000 and 200 transactions. A platform 1099-K reports gross volume, including shipping, tax and refunded orders, so it will be higher than your revenue; reconcile it before your accountant asks.

State entity compliance

Every state where your company is registered (home state and any foreign qualifications) has an annual or biennial report and often a franchise tax. Delaware LLCs pay their annual tax by June 1. Miss these and you can lose good standing, which can block bank accounts, contracts and lawsuits.

Marketing rules

  • SMS (TCPA). Damages run $500 to $1,500 per message. Since April 2025 the FCC requires honoring opt-outs made by "any reasonable means" within 10 business days, not just a reply of STOP. Check that your SMS tool actually honored last month's opt-outs.
  • Reviews. The FTC's rule on fake reviews and testimonials (in force since October 2024) bans buying reviews, suppressing negative ones and undisclosed insider reviews, with civil penalties per violation.
  • "Made in USA" and influencer disclosures are FTC territory too. Check claims before a campaign goes live.
  • Subscriptions. The FTC's click-to-cancel rule was struck down in 2025, but ROSCA enforcement and state auto-renewal laws (California's is among the strictest) still apply.

Website and privacy

  • Accessibility. E-commerce sites, including many Shopify stores, receive a large share of ADA website lawsuits. Overlay widgets don't prevent them. Test theme and popup changes with a keyboard and a screen reader.
  • State privacy laws. About 20 states now have one, with different thresholds; several require honoring browser opt-out signals (Global Privacy Control). Keep your privacy policy and consent banner current.
  • Payments. Shopify's hosted checkout keeps most stores in the simplest PCI category; custom scripts on payment pages can change that.

Product compliance

  • California Prop 65: if a warning applies, it must be shown online before purchase.
  • Cosmetics (MoCRA): facility registration and product listing with the FDA, plus adverse-event records.
  • Food and supplements: FDA food facility registrations renew between October 1 and December 31 of every even-numbered year, including 2026.
  • Children's products: a Children's Product Certificate from a CPSC-accepted lab. Amazon suspends listings without one.
  • Packaging EPR: Oregon, Colorado, California, Maine, Minnesota, Maryland and Washington have packaging laws that can make you a "producer" with registration, reporting and fees.

Marketplace identity and trademarks

Under the INFORM Consumers Act, marketplaces must suspend high-volume sellers who don't verify their information within 10 days of a request. And keep your trademark alive: a USPTO Section 8 declaration is due between years five and six after registration, or Amazon Brand Registry can lapse with it.

Rules that no longer apply

Stale advice is a cost too. Beneficial ownership (BOI) reporting no longer applies to US-formed companies after FinCEN's 2025 change, yet many pages still warn about it. Ask your accountant to confirm which rules apply to you before you pay someone to file something you don't need.
