What is Femp?

Femp (also called Femp AI, made by Neobrix) is an AI operations assistant for owners of Shopify and Amazon brands. It watches stock, ad spend against real sales, how findable your products are on Google and in AI assistants like ChatGPT, and your store's inbox. When something needs you, it messages you on WhatsApp or in the Femp app with the fix ready, and makes the change once you say yes.

Who is Femp for?

Owners and small teams running a direct-to-consumer brand on Shopify, Amazon or both, who don't have a full-time operations person and lose money when stock, ads, disputes or search slip during a busy week.

What does Femp connect to?

Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Search Console and Gmail, each through the platform's own approval screen. You talk to Femp on WhatsApp or in the Femp app. See integrations.

How much does Femp cost?

Femp is free during the beta, with every feature included. See pricing.

Can Femp change my store without asking?

No. Femp never touches prices, stock or checkout, and its ads access is read-only. It edits product SEO (titles, descriptions, image alt text) and blog posts only after you reply yes, and every edit can be undone.

How do I set up Femp?

Say hi on WhatsApp, open the link Femp sends, and connect your store. Your first stock check arrives a few minutes later. See how it works.

What reports does Femp send?

The ones you set up. Tell Femp what to watch and when, such as "check stock every morning at 9" or "every Monday, send me last week's profit", and it runs that automation in your time zone. Start from ready-made automations for sales, ads, pixel health, SEO, stock, chargebacks and the inbox, or describe your own. If nothing needs you, Femp stays quiet.

Does Femp help my products show up in ChatGPT and Google?

Yes. Femp scores each store's findability from 0 to 100 across Google, marketplaces and AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, researches fixes on the web, and, after your yes, rewrites product titles, descriptions, alt text and blog posts. See how to get products found on Google and in ChatGPT.

How is Femp different from a Shopify analytics dashboard?

A dashboard waits for you to open it and work out what matters. Femp checks the numbers for you every day, tells you only when something needs action, explains it in plain words and has the fix ready. It also reads sources a dashboard doesn't combine, such as ad spend, Amazon FBA stock and your store's inbox.

How is Femp different from hiring a virtual assistant?

Femp watches the store every day, including weekends, answers immediately and costs nothing during the beta. It doesn't replace judgement: you decide, and it only acts on what you approve. Many owners use Femp so a human teammate can spend time on customers and suppliers instead of checking numbers.

Is my data safe?

Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256, Femp never keeps passwords or one-time codes, it reads buyers' order history but not their names, emails or addresses, chat history is deleted after 90 days, and you can delete your account in one step. See security.

Can my team use Femp?

Yes. Add a teammate and they chat with Femp from their own WhatsApp. Femp remembers the store's preferences, so nobody repeats themselves.
