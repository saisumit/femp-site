What is Femp?

Femp stands for First EMPloyee. It's a general AI assistant you text on WhatsApp or use in the Femp app: ask questions, draft replies, sum up documents, set reminders. Install a plugin and it becomes your employee for that job, with the right connections and a daily routine. It's made by Femp AI, Inc.

What is a plugin?

A job you give Femp. Each plugin adds what that job needs: the accounts to connect, what to watch, and the automations that run in your time zone. Install one by texting Femp, for example “install femp for jobs”, or from the Plugins tab in the app.

Which plugins are there?

Femp for DTC brands (in beta) runs operations for Shopify and Amazon stores. Femp for Jobs and Femp for Finance are coming soon. You can suggest the next one on the waitlist.

Can I use Femp without a plugin?

Yes. On its own, Femp is a helpful assistant on WhatsApp. Plugins add a job on top: connections, a routine and check-ins.

How much does Femp cost?

Femp is free during the beta, with every plugin included. Everyone on the waitlist gets $100 in free credits. See pricing.

Does Femp act without asking?

No. Anything that sends a message, posts, pays or changes something waits for your yes. Femp shows what it will do first, and every change it makes is logged and can be undone where the platform allows it.

Is my data safe?

Every connection uses the platform's own sign-in, access tokens are encrypted with AES-256, Femp never keeps passwords or one-time codes, chat history is deleted after 90 days, and you can delete your account in one step. See security.

How do I start?

Say hi to Femp on WhatsApp, or join the waitlist and tell us which plugin you want. You can talk to Femp right away; plugins open as they launch.

Can my team use Femp?

Yes. Add a teammate and they chat with Femp from their own WhatsApp. Femp remembers shared preferences, so nobody repeats themselves.

What does Femp for DTC brands connect to?

Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Search Console and Gmail, each through the platform's own approval screen. It never touches prices, stock or checkout, and its ads access is read-only. See Femp for DTC brands.

Will Femp for Jobs apply to jobs for me?

No. It finds roles, tailors your resume and cover letter, and drafts emails in your Gmail. You review and send every application yourself.

Is Femp for Finance financial advice?

No. It sums up public information about the stocks you follow and reminds you about bills and subscriptions. It never places trades or tells you what to buy or sell.
