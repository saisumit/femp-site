What Femp will and won't do

  • Never touches your prices, stock or checkout.
  • Never edits your ads. Meta Ads and Google Ads access is read-only.
  • Changes only what you approve. Product SEO and blog edits happen after your yes, and every edit can be undone.
  • Drafts emails, doesn't send them. You send replies from your own inbox.

Your customers' data

  • Femp reads buyers' order history, not their names, emails or addresses.
  • Answers are worked out from your store's data for you and your team only.

Connections and credentials

  • Every connection uses the platform's own sign-in and approval screen (Shopify, Amazon, Meta, Google).
  • Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256.
  • Femp never keeps passwords or one-time codes.
  • Disconnect any connection from the app at any time. See integrations.

Retention and deletion

  • Chat history is deleted after 90 days.
  • Delete your account from the app in one step.

The full policy is in the privacy policy. Questions: support@femp.ai.
