1. Say hi on WhatsApp

Message Femp on WhatsApp. It replies with a personal link to connect your store. Femp is free during the beta.

2. Connect your tools through their own approval screens

Each connection uses the platform's official sign-in and consent screen: Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Search Console and Gmail. Femp never sees or keeps your passwords or one-time codes. Connect only what you want, and disconnect any of them from the app at any time. Details on the integrations page.

3. Get your first stock check in minutes

As soon as your store is connected, Femp runs a stock check and sends what's out, what's running low and how many days each product has left.

4. Set up your automations

Tell Femp what to watch and when, in your own words. Pick from ready-made automations or describe your own:

You sayWhat you get
"Every morning at 9, check stock"What's out, what runs out within a week at today's pace, and whether it's still advertised
"Review my ads every day before lunch"Yesterday's Meta and Google spend against real Shopify sales, with the budget change to make
"Tell me if my pixel misses sales"A message the day Meta or Google stop counting purchases your store took
"Watch my Google and ChatGPT rankings"Where you rank, what slipped, and the pages to rewrite
"Check the inbox every hour"Chargebacks, supplier delays, account warnings and reviews from your store's Gmail, with replies drafted
"Every Monday, send me last week's profit"Sales minus ad spend, fees and refunds, by product

Every automation runs on your store's real data, in your time zone. Change or stop one any time by saying so. If it finds nothing worth your time, Femp doesn't message you.

5. Talk it through, then Femp does it

When Femp finds something, it explains it in plain words and suggests what to do, like a teammate would. Ask questions, push back or change the plan; once you give the go-ahead, it handles it and tells you when it's done. Edits can be undone, and Femp never touches your prices, stock or checkout.

6. Ask anything, any time

Ask Femp about the store in plain words whenever you want: "how many orders today?", "which ads lost money last week?", "what's still to ship?". Teammates you add can ask too, from their own WhatsApp.
