1. Say hi on WhatsApp

Message Femp on WhatsApp. It replies with a personal link to connect your store. Femp is free during the beta.

2. Connect your tools through their own approval screens

Each connection uses the platform's official sign-in and consent screen: Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Search Console and Gmail. Femp never sees or keeps your passwords or one-time codes. Connect only what you want, and disconnect any of them from the app at any time. Details on the integrations page.

3. Get your first stock check in minutes

As soon as your store is connected, Femp runs a stock check and sends what's out, what's running low and how many days each product has left.

4. Femp works a shift every day

Every report runs on your store's real data, in your time zone. If a check finds nothing worth your time, Femp doesn't message you.

5. You say yes, Femp does it

When Femp suggests a change it can make, such as a better product title or a blog post, it shows you exactly what will change and waits. Reply yes and it's done; every edit can be undone. Femp never touches your prices, stock or checkout, and never edits your ads.

6. Ask anything, any time

Between check-ins, ask Femp about the store in plain words: "how many orders today?", "which ads lost money last week?", "what's still to ship?". Say "every Monday at 9, review my ads" and it becomes a standing job. Teammates you add can ask too, from their own WhatsApp.
