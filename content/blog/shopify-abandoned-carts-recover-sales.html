Baymard Institute's running average of published studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70%. You won't get that to zero, and you shouldn't try: many carts are window shopping. But the buyers who reached checkout and stopped are the warmest people your store will ever see, and you already paid to bring them there.

● In short
  • Fix the reasons first. Surprise costs at checkout are the top reason buyers leave.
  • Then recover: a reminder within an hour, a second the next day, and a discount only at the end, if at all.
  • Watch which products get abandoned most. A pattern there is a price or shipping problem.

Abandoned cart or abandoned checkout?

They're different in Shopify. A cart is anyone who added a product. A checkout is someone who started paying and typed an email or phone number. Shopify can only follow up on the second kind, and lists them under Orders → Abandoned checkouts. Reaching people who left from the cart page needs a logged-in customer or a marketing app that captures their email earlier.

Why buyers leave

Baymard's surveys of shoppers who abandoned a checkout keep finding the same reasons near the top:

  • Extra costs. Shipping, taxes or fees that appear only at checkout. This is the top reason by a wide margin.
  • Being asked to create an account.
  • Delivery too slow, or no delivery date shown.
  • Not trusting the site with card details.
  • A long or confusing checkout.
  • An unclear returns policy.

Fix the checkout first

  1. Show shipping early. Put the cost, or the free-shipping threshold, on the product page and in the cart.
  2. Allow guest checkout. In Shopify's customer account settings, keep accounts optional.
  3. Turn on express payments. Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal skip most of the form.
  4. Show a delivery estimate on the product page, not only at the end.
  5. Put the returns policy and reviews near the Add to cart button.

A recovery sequence that doesn't nag

WhenWhat to send
Within 1 hourA plain reminder with the product photo and a link back to their checkout. No discount.
About 24 hoursAnswer the likely objection: delivery time, returns, reviews from other buyers.
48–72 hoursOptional: a small incentive, ideally only for first-time buyers, so regulars don't learn to wait for a code.

Shopify's Marketing → Automations has an abandoned checkout flow you can edit. Only send SMS to people who agreed to texts at checkout; see the SMS consent rules in our compliance calendar.

Measure it weekly

  • Recovery rate: recovered orders divided by abandoned checkouts that left an email or phone.
  • Abandoned by product: one product abandoned far more than others usually means its shipping cost or price looks wrong at checkout.
  • Abandoned by step: buyers leaving at shipping point to cost. Leaving at payment points to trust or payment options.
