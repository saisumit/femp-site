Femp / DTC brands / Features

What Femp does for your store

Femp is an AI operations assistant for Shopify and Amazon brands. It watches the parts of the store that slip when you're busy and tells you on WhatsApp when something needs you, with the fix ready.

● In short
  • Stock: live levels from Shopify and Amazon FBA, with days of cover for each product.
  • Ads: Meta and Google spend checked against what your store actually sold. Read-only.
  • Findability: a 0–100 score across Google, marketplaces and AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, plus fixes you approve.
  • Inbox: chargebacks, supplier delays, account warnings and reviews flagged from your store's Gmail, with replies drafted.
  • Social (coming soon): daily stories and short videos made from your store, posted to every channel in one tap.
  • Orders and team: ask anything about sales and buyers, and add teammates who chat from their own WhatsApp.

Stock: days of cover, not just counts

Femp reads live inventory from Shopify and Amazon FBA and works out how many days each product lasts at its current pace. You set your own alert limits. A daily stock automation lists what's out, what runs out within a week, and whether you're still paying for ads on something that's about to sell out.

Why days of cover matters: "40 left" sounds fine until you sell nine a day. Read more in Days of cover: the stock number that stops your best seller selling out.

Ads: spend checked against real sales

Meta and Google each take credit for the same sale. Femp compares yesterday's spend in Meta Ads and Google Ads with the orders your Shopify store actually took, so ad sets that lose money show up the next morning, together with the budget change Femp would make. Femp never edits your ads; access is read-only and you make the change yourself.

Background: Are your ads making money? Check ROAS against real store sales.

Findability: found on Google and in ChatGPT

More shoppers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers what to buy. Femp gives each store a 0–100 findability score across Google, marketplaces and AI assistants, then researches three fresh ideas on the web every afternoon. Reply with a number and Femp rewrites the product title, description, image alt text or a blog post. Every edit waits for your yes and can be undone.

The checklist behind it: How to get your products found on Google and in ChatGPT.

Inbox: the emails that matter, flagged

Once you connect your store's Gmail, Femp checks it every hour for chargebacks, supplier delays, marketplace account warnings and reviews. It tells you which ones need you and drafts the replies for you to send. A chargeback with a deadline doesn't get buried under newsletters.

On disputes: Shopify chargebacks: how to answer every dispute before the deadline.

Social: stories from your store, on every channel (coming soon)

Most owners run their own social and go quiet when the store gets busy. Femp will hand you two or three finished stories each morning, made from what happened in your store: a restock, a new review, a launch, a best seller, plus angles from what competitors and Amazon alternatives are posting. One tap posts a story to Instagram and Facebook, then TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest, cropped to each channel's shape. Femp tracks which stories sold and offers to boost the winners as ads. Nothing posts without your yes.

The playbook behind it: Your store makes a story every day. Here's how to post it everywhere.

Orders: who's buying, and buying again

Ask in plain words: sales by day or week, new versus repeat buyers, best sellers, where orders came from, and what's still waiting to ship. Femp answers from your connected data and suggests a next step. It reads buyers' order history, not their names, emails or addresses.

Automations you set up in one message

Tell Femp what to watch and when, and it becomes an automation that runs in your time zone: "every morning at 9, check stock", "tell me if my pixel misses sales", "every Monday, send me last week's profit". Start from ready-made automations or describe your own. Femp stays quiet when nothing is wrong. See how it works.

Team and memory

Add a teammate and they chat with Femp from their own WhatsApp: the person who packs orders can ask what's still to ship. Femp remembers your preferences, so nobody has to repeat them.
