Femp / Security

Security and ground rules

Femp works for you, and you stay in charge. These are the rules it follows with every plugin and your data.

For every plugin

  • Acts only after your yes. Sending, posting, paying or changing anything waits for your approval.
  • Drafts emails, doesn't send them unless you say so. You send from your own inbox.
  • Never places trades and never tells you what to buy or sell.

Femp for DTC brands

  • Never touches your prices, stock or checkout.
  • Never edits your ads. Meta Ads and Google Ads access is read-only.
  • Changes only what you approve. Product SEO and blog edits happen after your yes, and every edit can be undone.

Your data

  • For stores, Femp reads buyers' order history, not their names, emails or addresses.
  • Answers are worked out from your store's data for you and your team only.

Connections and credentials

  • Every connection uses the platform's own sign-in and approval screen (Shopify, Amazon, Meta, Google and others).
  • Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256.
  • Femp never keeps passwords or one-time codes.
  • Disconnect any connection from the app at any time.

Retention and deletion

  • Chat history is deleted after 90 days.
  • Delete your account from the app in one step.

The full policy is in the privacy policy. Questions: support@femp.ai.
